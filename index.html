// case study

Proving a returned piece was really sold

The problem

When a customer brings a gold piece back for exchange, any store has to be able to confirm it was really sold by the company, from its tag or HUID and its exact weights, even for older sales. The sales history comes out of the company's ERP (Synergics) as export files.

What I built

  • An import flow for the ERP exports (CSV or Excel): column matching, a preview, warnings before anything is replaced, and one-click undo.
  • 93,800+ bills and 38,600+ customers migrated from the legacy exports.
  • A counter screen: find a sold piece by who bought it (phone, HUID or name) or by describing the piece. Every search is recorded.
  • Four user roles, with per-user limits on which networks someone can sign in from.
  • Customer notes, a contact log and follow-ups for the sales team.
  • Covered by 123 unit tests and 50 end-to-end tests.

The result

Live in production since October 2026. Stores can search the full imported sales history by tag, HUID, phone or weight.
